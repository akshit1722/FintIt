Frequently Asked Questions

What proof do I need to collect an item?
Bring a valid student or staff ID card and be prepared to describe specific identifying markings or unlock electronic screens.
How long are found items stored?
Unclaimed items are retained securely in campus custody for 60 calendar days before standard semester disposition.
